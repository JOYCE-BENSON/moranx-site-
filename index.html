Who We Are

A YOUNG FIRM,
BUILT THE RIGHT WAY

Moranx Construction Limited was established as a road works contracting firm serving Narok County and beyond. We're a new company — and we secured our National Construction Authority registration and practising licence before taking on work, rather than the other way around.

That means every quotation, every site visit, and every conversation with us starts from a verifiable position: a real registration number, a real category, and a real person with engineering qualifications behind the work.

Moranx is also a family business, founded by a father and son. Geoffrey Leitato Totona, our Managing Director, is a qualified civil engineer who leads technical delivery and site supervision on every project we take on. His father, Kiplagat Kimaiywa Totona, serves as Director and shareholder, bringing three decades of business experience to how the company is run. Between them, hands-on engineering discipline meets long-standing business judgment — and a shared name on the door means a shared stake in getting every job right.

We're not the biggest contractor in the room. We intend to be the most straightforward one to work with.

What We Do

ROAD WORKS, WITHIN
OUR REGISTERED SCOPE

Service areas covered under our NCA8 Road Works registration.

ROAD CONSTRUCTION & GRADING
  • Construction of murram and gravel roads
  • Grading, formation and compaction
  • Access road development for rural and urban sites
  • Site preparation and road alignment
MAINTENANCE & REHABILITATION
  • Routine road maintenance
  • Periodic rehabilitation of worn surfaces
  • Pothole patching and resurfacing
  • Scheduled inspection and upkeep
DRAINAGE & CULVERT WORKS
  • Design and installation of road drainage systems
  • Culvert construction and installation
  • Erosion control measures
  • Stormwater management along road corridors
EARTHWORKS & SITE CLEARANCE
  • Site clearance and vegetation removal
  • Cut-and-fill earthworks
  • Excavation and grading
  • Land preparation ahead of road works
PROJECT SUPERVISION
  • On-site supervision led by a qualified civil engineer
  • Quality control and compliance monitoring
  • Progress reporting and documentation
  • Coordination with subcontractors and suppliers
SUPPLIES & PROCUREMENT
  • Supply and delivery of general hardware and equipment
  • Electrical fittings and fixtures
  • Plumbing and sanitary supplies
  • Public procurement and tender fulfilment — see our track record below

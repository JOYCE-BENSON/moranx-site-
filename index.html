Who We Are

A YOUNG FIRM,
BUILT THE RIGHT WAY ROUND

Moranx Construction Limited was established as a road works contracting firm serving Narok County and beyond. We're a new company — and we secured our National Construction Authority registration and practising licence before taking on work, rather than the other way around.

That means every quotation, every site visit, and every conversation with us starts from a verifiable position: a real registration number, a real category, and a real person with engineering qualifications behind the work. We're not the biggest contractor in the room. We intend to be the most straightforward one to work with.

What We Do

ROAD WORKS, WITHIN
OUR REGISTERED SCOPE

Service areas covered under our NCA8 Road Works registration.

ROAD CONSTRUCTION & GRADING
Construction and grading of murram and gravel roads, including formation and compaction.
MAINTENANCE & REHABILITATION
Routine and periodic maintenance to keep existing road surfaces safe and passable.
DRAINAGE & CULVERT WORKS
Installation of drainage structures and culverts to protect road works from water damage.
EARTHWORKS & SITE CLEARANCE
Clearance, cut-and-fill, and earthworks preparation ahead of road construction.
MATERIALS & SUPPLIES
Sourcing and delivery of goods and materials, drawing on our public procurement track record.
PROJECT SUPERVISION
On-site supervision led by our Managing Director, a qualified civil engineer.
